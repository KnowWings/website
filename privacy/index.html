Privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains, in plain language, how KnowWings handles information shared through this website and through direct communication with us.

Information you choose to provide

If you contact KnowWings by email or send us information in connection with an enquiry, collaboration, project or career opportunity, we receive the information you choose to provide. This may include your name, email address, company or institution, professional background, CV, project details and the content of your message.

Technical information

Our website hosting and infrastructure providers may process standard technical information required to deliver and secure the website, such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, timestamps and server logs.

How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and communicate with you.
  • To assess potential projects, collaborations, expert-network participation or career opportunities.
  • To operate, maintain and secure the website and related services.
  • To meet legal, regulatory or contractual obligations where applicable.

Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that support our website, communications or business operations, or shared where required by law. For project or expert-network enquiries, relevant information may also be shared internally or with appropriate collaborators when necessary to evaluate or carry out the requested work.

Retention and security

We aim to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was provided, business record-keeping, or applicable legal requirements. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no online system or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have provided to KnowWings, or to request correction or deletion where appropriate and legally permitted.

External services and links

This website may rely on third-party hosting or infrastructure services and may link to external websites. Those third parties may have their own privacy practices, which are governed by their respective policies.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as the website, our services or applicable requirements change. The version published on this page is the current version.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact contact@knowwings.com.
