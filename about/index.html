About KnowWings

Extending the reach of internal R&D.

KnowWings is building a collaborative R&D intelligence model that helps organizations access the research, expertise and external capabilities needed beyond their internal teams.

What we believe

The problem should determine the research.

Important R&D questions rarely fit neatly into a standard research product. They may require technology investigation, scientific depth, supplier discovery, expert judgment, direct validation—or a combination of all five.

We build each engagement around that need, bringing together the right evidence and expertise to help the client move forward with greater confidence.

Operating principles

Simple standards for serious research.

Problem before methodStart with the question and work backward to the evidence.
Depth where neededBring specialist knowledge only where it strengthens the answer.
Evidence before confidenceSeparate established facts, informed inference and uncertainty.
Action over accumulationFocus on intelligence that can change a decision or next step.

Our direction

Internal focus. External reach.

Our capabilities today are the foundation for a broader vision: enabling organizations to combine focused internal R&D capabilities with the best external expertise, research and infrastructure available.

We are developing a distributed network of researchers, PhD scholars, experienced domain experts and institutional relationships that can be assembled around specific technical and strategic questions.

Work with us

Bring us the R&D question that does not have an obvious answer.

We will help determine what needs to be understood, who needs to be engaged and how the critical claims can be validated.
