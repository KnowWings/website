About KnowWings

An engineering-led team connected to a wider specialist network.

KnowWings helps R&D and innovation teams investigate complex questions, identify external capabilities, validate critical claims, and make better-informed decisions.

The team

Engineering-led. Network-enabled.

KnowWings is led by an engineering-focused core team and supported by a network of researchers, PhD scholars, scientists, engineers, and industry specialists across technical and commercial domains.

For each engagement, we bring together the research capacity, specialist expertise, and primary intelligence required by the question, rather than relying on a fixed project team.

The Right Expertise for the Question

01Research capacity

Researchers and PhD scholars investigate scientific, technical, market, and competitive questions with depth.

02Specialist expertise

Scientists, engineers, and industry specialists help interpret evidence, challenge assumptions, and evaluate technical and commercial implications.

03Primary intelligence

Relevant experts, suppliers, technology providers, and industry participants can be engaged to validate claims and close critical evidence gaps.

Our direction

Internal focus. External reach.

Our aim is simple: make it easier for R&D teams to combine their internal knowledge with the right external research, expertise and capabilities when the problem demands it.

We continue to expand the specialist network and relationships behind this model, while keeping the focus on one outcome: giving teams a clearer basis for technical and strategic decisions.
