About KnowWings

A small core team building a wider R&D network.

KnowWings is developing a collaborative model for helping organizations reach beyond the limits of their internal R&D teams when a problem requires additional research, expertise or external capability.

The team

Engineering-led. Network-enabled.

Led by a small team of engineers, KnowWings is building a growing network of researchers, PhD scholars and vetted domain experts—including retired scientists and engineers from industry and research backgrounds.

Alongside this network, we are developing relationships with leading Indian research institutions to create a practical bridge between focused internal R&D teams and the specialized knowledge, people and capabilities available outside their organizations.

What we are building

01Research capacity

Researchers and PhD scholars who can investigate scientific, technical and market questions with depth.

02Experienced judgment

Vetted specialists, including retired scientists and engineers, who can pressure-test findings with practical experience.

03Institutional access

Developing relationships with Indian research institutions to broaden access to specialist knowledge and research capability.

Our direction

Internal focus. External reach.

Our aim is simple: make it easier for R&D teams to combine their internal knowledge with the right external research, expertise and capabilities when the problem demands it.

We are building this network progressively, around real problems and real technical needs—not as a directory of experts, but as an operating model for collaborative R&D intelligence.
