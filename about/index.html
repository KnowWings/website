About KnowWings

Built for questions where a quick answer is not enough.

KnowWings is an early-stage research and decision-intelligence consultancy for R&D and innovation teams navigating technical and commercial uncertainty.

The client is not ultimately buying research.

They are buying greater confidence in an important decision.

That belief shapes the company we are building: decision-first, technically curious, transparent about uncertainty and willing to validate the claims that matter.

Our ambition is to become a trusted intelligence partner, supporting both urgent questions and the continuous awareness R&D and innovation teams need over time.

Operating principles

How we intend to earn trust.

Question before method

Start with the decision and work backward to the evidence.

Depth where needed

Bring specialist knowledge into the areas that demand it.

Evidence before confidence

Distinguish sourced fact, informed inference and unresolved uncertainty.

Action over accumulation

Prioritize the intelligence that can change a choice or next step.

Specialist network

A flexible intelligence team around each question.

KnowWings selects scientific, technical and industry expertise based on the question, the evidence required and the decision the client needs to make.
