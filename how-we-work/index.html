Five stages

A disciplined path from question to action.

Each engagement can use a different mix of methods, but the logic remains consistent.

01

Frame the problem

Clarify the technical or strategic question, the decision behind it and the evidence required.

02

Investigate

Build the evidence base across literature, patents, databases, market sources and relevant technical information.

03

Deepen

Bring in researchers, PhD scholars and experienced specialists where deeper scientific, engineering or commercial judgment is needed.

04

Validate

Test important claims and assumptions through expert engagement, supplier conversations and primary outreach.

05

Synthesize

Integrate the evidence, expose uncertainty and translate the findings into implications, options and next steps.
