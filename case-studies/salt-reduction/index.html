Anonymized case study · Food & beverage

Less salt.
A clearer path
to testing.

An F&B company wanted to reduce salt without losing the qualities customers expect. KnowWings turned a broad formulation question into a focused shortlist and practical trial roadmap.

4product platforms considered
6specialists interviewed
18companies contacted

The challenge

Reduce salt. Protect the full product.

Salt influences flavour, structure, preservation and manufacturing consistency. The question was which ingredient or process combinations were credible enough for controlled trials.

How we narrowed the field

Evidence → validation → decisions.

01

Map the options

02

Test the claims

Use expert interviews and supplier outreach to check fit and feasibility.

03

Prioritize trials

Filter by evidence, product fit, cost and implementation risk.

The testing roadmap

Three routes. One disciplined plan.

NEAR-TERM

Trial available options

Prioritize commercially ready ingredients with relevant evidence and support.

COMBINATIONS

Balance trade-offs

Pair reduction with flavour or functional support where one option is insufficient.

LONGER-TERM

Monitor emerging ideas

Revisit promising approaches as evidence, availability and scale improve.

The outcome

A roadmap the R&D team could act on.

The company received a basis for deciding what to test, in which combinations and against which criteria. Product performance remains subject to trials.
