Building a practical salt-reduction roadmap for bakery products.

The challenge

Salt reduction was not simply a taste problem.

An international bakery manufacturer wanted to move below 1% salt while protecting the characteristics that make products commercially viable: taste, dough behaviour, texture, shelf life, manufacturing consistency and consumer acceptance.

Internal reformulation had already delivered progress. The next reductions required a wider view of ingredients, processing technologies and combinations that could address sodium reduction without creating new technical problems elsewhere.

Recommendation logic

Move from research to controlled testing — without betting on one universal replacement.

1. Shortlist & sample

Obtain samples from focused solution providers rather than expanding the landscape indefinitely.

2. Test alone & in combinations

Compare single technologies with combinations that address both taste and functional roles.

3. Test across platforms

Evaluate fresh, frozen and ambient products rather than assuming one solution transfers cleanly.

4. Measure the full system

Track sensory response, dough behaviour, shelf life, water activity and manufacturing performance.

5. Compare cost vs benefit

Balance technical performance against formulation cost, implementation complexity and scale.

6. Keep simple reduction as a benchmark

For some products, incremental reduction may outperform a costly replacement system economically.
