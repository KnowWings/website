Careers at KnowWings

Bring expertise, curiosity and technical depth to real R&D problems.

KnowWings is building a flexible network of experienced specialists and emerging technical researchers who can contribute to focused research, validation and problem-solving assignments.

01 / SUBJECT MATTER EXPERTS

Experienced industry specialists.

We work with subject matter experts who bring deep practical knowledge from industry, engineering, science, technology and related domains.

  • 10+ years of relevant industry experience.
  • Strong domain knowledge and practical judgment.
  • Ability to review findings, validate technical claims and contribute specialist insight.
  • Engagements may be assignment-based, advisory or project-specific.
SME network
02 / SCHOLARS

Technical researchers and scholars.

We welcome people with strong technical foundations who want to work on structured research and real-world R&D intelligence projects.

  • Technical background or professional degree.
  • Master's, doctoral and PhD scholars are welcome.
  • Part-time and full-time opportunities may be available.
  • Work can involve technical research, evidence synthesis, market and technology investigation, and expert outreach.
